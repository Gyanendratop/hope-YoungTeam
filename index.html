The Circulation Chain

Four stages. One uninterrupted flow.

Like a circulatory system, supply doesn't pause at any single point — it moves continuously from source to bedside. This is how Hope YoungTeam keeps it moving.

STAGE 01

Sourcing

Manufacturer and wholesale partnerships, including direct outreach to overseas equipment producers for bulk pricing.

STAGE 02

Hope YoungTeam Hub

Central intake in Kathmandu — quality checks, inventory holding, and order consolidation before dispatch.

STAGE 03

Regional Distribution

Dispatch to sub-distributors and regional partners across Nepal's provinces, keeping lead times short.

STAGE 04

Point of Care

Final delivery, installation support, and after-sales service directly to clinics and hospitals.
